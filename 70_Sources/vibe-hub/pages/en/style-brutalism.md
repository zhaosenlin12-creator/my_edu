Design Principles

- Make the structure and interaction obvious
- Use strong type and borders with a clear hierarchy
- Choose a limited high-contrast palette
- Let hard shadows describe layers consistently
- Keep intentional irregularity separate from accidental disorder

### When to use it

- Creative studio or portfolio

  Stop overthinking. Ship it.An indie developer’s defiance and directness
- Youth, culture, and event campaigns

  E-book ¥29Gumroad-style digital goods
- A product with a direct, playful voice

  Hackathon 202648 hours
- Small sites that benefit from strong identity

  “Anti-polish” is a stance too

### When NOT to use it

- Use constant visual shouting in a dense work tool

  Banking app (thick black outlines + fluorescent pink)Finance and healthcare need trust; a brutalist treatment can feel unreliable.
- Sacrifice reading and focus states for attitude

  Enterprise tools need credibility, scanability, and clear states first.
- Mix many unrelated colors, borders, and shadows

  Government service portal (high-contrast palette)Formal service contexts should prioritize dignity, stability, and ease of use.
- Imitate roughness without a consistent layout
