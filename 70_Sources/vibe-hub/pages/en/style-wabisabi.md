Design Principles

- Build a low-stimulation palette from real materials and imagery
- Use space to slow the reading pace without losing structure
- Choose type for the language and content
- Create balanced asymmetry rather than random placement
- Keep paper, clay, and grain textures behind the reading layer

### When to use it

- Home and craft brand

  Natural linen bedding · 4-piece setA home-goods brand site can use restrained materials and typography.
- Tea, fragrance, and hand-made goods

  Handmade stoneware cups · Fired one kiln at a timeE-commerce for tea, incense, and tableware
- Hospitality and travel site

  Mountain hot-spring inn · Check the booking page for live availabilityA guesthouse booking page can use a quiet visual rhythm to present the space.
- Essay or personal blog focused on long reading

  "Autumn Memo" · Prose CollectionPersonal blogs and long-form reading

### When NOT to use it

- Reduce scan efficiency in a data-heavy tool

  Data dashboard: multiple charts + live KPI updatesIn task-dense contexts, prioritize scanability.
- Add saturated accents with no clear role

  +
- Use large empty areas without careful type and material detail

  One line of text on a plain white background, with no relationship to the contentWhitespace should support reading or showcase material, not stand in for information.
- Interrupt the quiet rhythm with constant promotion labels

  50% off for a limited timeBestsellerNew arrival

  Too many promotional tags interrupt the reading rhythm.
