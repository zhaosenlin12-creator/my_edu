# Footer

You might say

We need a footer with a few links and the copyright information.

**End the page with useful links, identity, and required information**·A footer gives visitors another route to important pages and carries company details, legal links, contact information, or a final action. Organize links into clear groups and keep it useful rather than turning it into a second copy of the entire site.
