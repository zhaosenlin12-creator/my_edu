# Gradient

You might say

Instead of a solid background, let the color fade from blue into purple.

**Blend colors gradually across an area**·A gradient moves smoothly between two or more colors. It can add depth, direction, or emphasis to a background and illustration. Keep text contrast stable across the whole blend, and use a flat color when the gradient adds no meaning.
