# Alert

You might say

Keep a yellow warning at the top of the page to let people know their account is about to expire.

**Keep an important message or warning visible on the page**·An alert sits near the related content and explains a status, risk, or problem without blocking the whole page. Use a heading, icon, and clear wording together to show importance; color alone is not enough.

### When NOT to use it

- Interrupt a small successful action

  ✓ Saved successfullyA one-line success message doesn’t need to stay on screen
- Show a vague warning with no next step

  ⚠ Deletion needs an explicit confirmation

- Use red for every informational message
