# Tooltip

You might say

When someone hovers over this icon, show a short label explaining what it does.

**Explain a compact control with a short text label**·A tooltip gives a brief label or hint for an icon or unfamiliar control. It should appear on keyboard focus as well as hover and must not contain essential instructions. If the content is longer or interactive, use a [Popover](/en/popover).
