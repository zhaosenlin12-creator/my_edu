Add a few real customer quotes instead of just saying we're great.

**Use real customer words to show a concrete experience**·A customer quote can explain what changed for a real user in language that feels more specific than product claims. Keep the wording accurate, identify the source with permission, and choose quotes that add evidence rather than generic praise.

### When NOT to use it

- Use praise with no specific detail
