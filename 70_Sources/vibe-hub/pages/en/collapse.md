# Collapse

You might say

There are a lot of questions. Show just the question first, then let people open it to see the answer.

**Hide secondary details until the user asks for them**·A collapse panel shows a heading first and expands the body on request. It suits FAQs and advanced settings. Do not hide warnings, required rules, or anything people must see before they act.

Collapse**≠**Accordion

A collapse panel is any content that can expand and close. An accordion is usually a group of collapse panels and often allows only one open panel at a time.
