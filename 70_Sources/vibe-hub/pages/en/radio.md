# Radio

You might say

These options should be mutually exclusive—choosing one should clear the last one.

**Choose exactly one option from a small set**·Radio buttons show mutually exclusive choices such as payment method or visibility. Give the group a clear question and keep all options visible. If there are many options or users need to search, a [Select](/en/select) may work better.
