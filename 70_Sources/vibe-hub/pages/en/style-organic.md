# Organic Design

You might say

Give it a more natural feel with earth tones, rounded shapes, and a handmade touch.

**Create warmth through natural material, real people, and a gentler pace**·Organic design uses flowing curves, irregular shapes, material texture, and earth colors drawn from nature, contrasting with strictly geometric form. It often appears in food, wellness, home, and sustainability-oriented brands, but organic describes a formal visual language and does not automatically mean environmentally responsible. Product origin, service promises, and the next action still need specific expression.

Design Principles

- **Take color from real material and place**: connect the palette to the product, land, room, or people
- **Prioritize photography over texture**: real processes, hands, and environments create believable warmth
- **Keep hierarchy inside the gentle pace**: serif headlines, body text, and actions cannot all fade into softness
- **Make origin and promises specific**: place, material, quantity, and method are more credible than natural alone

### When to use it

- **Food, wellness, fragrance, and home brands** where material and use context are part of the value

  FIELD NOTE 04**Grown slowly, made simply.**Traceable ingredients
- **Small-batch craft and sustainable products** whose origin and process can lead the story

  ⌁

  **Small-batch care**See the product page for current origin and batch details.
- **Hospitality, restaurants, and slow travel** where real space and people create expectation
- **Personal services and studios** that need warmth and care alongside expertise

### When NOT to use it

- **Turn every surface beige**; without material, content, and typographic relationships, the result is only flat

  **Beige without a story**Texture cannot replace useful information
- **Use natural and pure instead of facts**; explain material, source, and actual practice
- **Place texture over body text**; paper and grain belong behind the reading layer
- **Make the action disappear in the quiet mood**; purchase, booking, and contact still need clear affordance
