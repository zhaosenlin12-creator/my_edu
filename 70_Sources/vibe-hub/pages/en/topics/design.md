Design Styles22 entries

### Minimalism

Keep it clean and uncluttered, with the content and buttons doing the talking.

### Apple HIG

I want that polished Apple website feel: big images, big type, and lots of white space.

### Notion Style

Make it feel like Notion—clean, text-first, and inviting to write in.

### Bento Grid

Use one of those trendy feature sections made of differently sized tiles.

### Glassmorphism

Use translucent frosted-glass panels where you can faintly see the background through them.

### Neo-Brutalism

Go bold and expressive with thick black borders, bright color blocks, and hard shadows.

### Swiss Style

Use a strict grid, big type, and precise alignment so it feels clean like a poster.

### Editorial

Lay it out like a fashion magazine, with images and text woven together.

### Skeuomorphism

Give it that early iPhone look, where buttons look like real buttons and notes look like actual notebooks.

### Flat Design

No depth or shadows—just solid colors and simple shapes.

### Material Design

Give it an Android-style look with layered cards and a ripple when people tap.

### Neumorphism

Make the buttons look like they're softly raised out of the background in the same color palette.

### SaaS Marketing Site

Make it look like a software company website: a big hero headline, product screenshots, customer logos, and pricing.

### B2B Corporate Site

This site is for business customers, so it needs to feel professional, steady, and trustworthy.

### DTC E-commerce

This is a brand's store, so it should have personality, tell a story, and make people want to buy.

### Dark UI

Give it a high-tech look with a black background, like a code editor.

### Playful Illustration

Make it more playful with fun illustrations and characters. Don't make it too serious.

### Organic Design

Give it a more natural feel with earth tones, rounded shapes, and a handmade touch.

### Y2K

Give it a Y2K retro-future feel with metallics, bubblegum colors, and translucent plastic.

### Memphis

Use colorful geometric shapes, squiggles, and dots for an '80s feel.

### Terminal Aesthetic

Make it feel like a hacker terminal: black background, green text, monospace type, and command-line vibes.

### Wabi-sabi

Make it a little more understated and weathered, with lots of open space and a quiet sense of age.
