# Drawer

You might say

Open a details panel from the right, but keep the list behind it visible.

**Slide in a side panel without leaving the current page**·A drawer keeps the current page visible while showing details, filters, navigation, or a short editing task from the side. Make the boundary and close behavior clear. If the content needs a lot of space or has its own navigation, use a full page.
