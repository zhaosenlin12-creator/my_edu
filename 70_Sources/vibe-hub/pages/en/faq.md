# FAQ

You might say

Collect the questions people ask most often into a Q&A section at the bottom of the page.

**Answer common questions before visitors need to ask**·A FAQ section handles repeated questions about price, setup, policy, or fit. Write questions in the visitor's language and answer them directly. If the same question needs a long explanation, link to a full guide instead of hiding an article inside an accordion.

### When to use it

- Address purchase concerns

- Explain setup or policy

  Is the free version enough?Pick questions from real customer service records
- Reduce repeated support questions

- Clarify details that do not fit the main story

### When NOT to use it

- Invent questions nobody asks

  Why are we number one in the industry?
- Use the FAQ to hide essential product information

  How to get a refund?
- Write promotional answers that avoid the question

  Question oneQuestion 2Question three
- Place long documentation inside every answer

  Pricing and billingKey information only appears at the end of the FAQ, and the main decision-making locations are not explained.
