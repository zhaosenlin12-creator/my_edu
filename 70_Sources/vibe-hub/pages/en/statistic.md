# Statistic

You might say

Put a few big numbers on the homepage, like total users and sales.

**Highlight a small number of important values**·A statistic combines a label, value, unit, and sometimes a trend. If a number went up or down, say what time period or baseline it is compared with. Keep formats and definitions consistent for money, percentages, counts, and other units.
