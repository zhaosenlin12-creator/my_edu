# Doc Layout

You might say

Lay this out like documentation: navigation on the left, the article in the middle, and this page's outline on the right.

**Combine section navigation, article content, and an on-page outline**·A documentation layout often uses a left navigation for pages, a readable center column for the article, and a right outline for headings. Keep each column's job distinct and collapse secondary navigation on small screens.

### When NOT to use it

- Give all three columns equal visual weight

  ContentsBodyOutline
- Make the article column too wide to read

  Keeping three columns on mobile leaves too little width for the body.
- Show an outline that does not follow the headings
- Keep side columns fixed on a narrow screen

  1.11.21.32.12.22.33.13.2

  The table of contents is longer than the page itself
