# List

You might say

Just stack these items in a consistent list, one after another.

**Show similar items one after another**·A list repeats the same structure for messages, articles, contacts, or other items that people scan from top to bottom. It is more flexible than a table and more compact than a grid of cards, so it works when column-by-column comparison is not important.
