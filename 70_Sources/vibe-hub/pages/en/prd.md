# PRD

You might say

Help me spell out what I'm building for the AI: the problem it solves and what counts as done.

**A product document that defines the user problem, scope, and success criteria**·Before a team builds one-click meeting-notes export, a PRD explains who needs it, what this release includes, and how the team will know it worked. It aligns product, design, and engineering without locking every technical detail.
