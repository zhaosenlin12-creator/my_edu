# Hero

You might say

The top of the site feels empty. Give it a big image and headline so people immediately get what we do.

**Use the opening area to explain the page's main point and next step**·The Hero usually sits directly below the Header or Navbar. It may combine a headline, supporting copy, a primary action, and a product visual, but there is no required component checklist. What matters is that these elements explain the page's main value together. Customer logos, feature lists, and pricing are normally separate sections even when they appear nearby.
