# User Flow

You might say

Map out every step a user takes from arriving on the site to finishing checkout.

**A path diagram of the steps and branches a user takes to reach one goal**·A sign-up flow might show entering the sign-up page, filling in details, verification success or failure, and account creation. It keeps the steps that affect progress, not every click on the screen.
