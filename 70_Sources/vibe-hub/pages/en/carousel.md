# Carousel

You might say

I want several hero images to rotate automatically, with left and right arrows too.

**Show several panels one at a time in the same space**·A carousel often appears in promotional banners and onboarding, but later slides are easy to miss. Do not place essential information only inside it. If it moves automatically, let people pause it and provide clear controls and position indicators.

### When NOT to use it
