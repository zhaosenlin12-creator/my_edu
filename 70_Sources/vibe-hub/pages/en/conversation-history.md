You might say

Why do I have to send the earlier messages along with every new question?

**Earlier messages a product brings into a new request so the assistant can understand references**·When you say “make the second one shorter,” the product can bring back the relevant earlier titles. A visible chat transcript does not guarantee every old message was sent again.
