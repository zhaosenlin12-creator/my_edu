# Context Engineering

You might say

After a long conversation, the AI starts forgetting things and going off track. What information should I give it?

**Selecting, maintaining, and retrieving the information an AI agent needs for each turn**·For a long login-fix task, give the agent the error, relevant files, confirmed constraints, and latest test result—not every old log. Context engineering keeps the signal current and retrieves details when needed.
