# Playful Illustration

You might say

Make it more playful with fun illustrations and characters. Don't make it too serious.

**Use distinctive character, shape, and feedback to make a product feel friendly and alive**·Playful illustration is a visual direction that builds brand character through illustrated people, scenes, and exaggerated forms; it is more specific than using bright color or large rounded corners. It often pairs with expressive but clear typography and light motion, working well for learning, creative, and young consumer products. Illustration cannot excuse weak information or unclear interaction states.

Design Principles

- **Design one recognizable character voice**: color, type, illustration, and copy should express the same personality
- **Reserve exaggeration for emphasis**: a headline, success response, or hero illustration can lead while other areas stay calm
- **Rounded does not mean vague**: buttons, inputs, and states still need clear boundaries and labels
- **Let motion explain cause and effect**: completion, dragging, and progress can feel elastic without slowing the task

### When to use it

- **Learning, creator, and lightweight productivity products** that want a welcoming first experience

- **Younger consumer and community brands** where personality and warmth are meaningful differences

- **Products with a mascot or illustration system** that can support onboarding, empty states, and feedback
- **Tasks that commonly create anxiety**, where gentle language and positive response can reduce pressure

### When NOT to use it

- **Make every element bounce, rotate, and change color**; constant interruption erases priority

  **BOUNCE! POP! WOW!**Every element shouts at the same volume
- **Confuse childish treatment with friendliness**; adults enjoy lightness without being spoken down to
- **Use bright text with weak contrast**; vivid surfaces still need a stable reading layer
- **Answer serious outcomes only with emoji**; payment, access, and errors still require precise explanation
