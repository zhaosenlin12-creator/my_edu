# Responsive Design

You might say

At mobile width, change three columns to one while keeping the desktop navigation and card layout intact.

**Responsive design is the practice of adapting one interface's layout and interaction to available space and device capabilities.**·At 1440px, a site may show three card columns and full navigation; at 390px, it can use one column, larger touch targets, and collapsed secondary navigation. Breakpoints and media queries are common tools. The goal is not proportional desktop shrinkage but readable, tappable, completable tasks.
