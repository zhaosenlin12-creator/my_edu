# Quote

You might say

Pull out this person's exact words and make it stand out, with their name attached.

**A sourced quotation or excerpt set apart from the surrounding text**·Use indentation, quotation marks, or a rule to separate an expert’s words or customer feedback from an author’s view. Keep the speaker, source, or needed context; do not style ordinary text as a quote just for decoration.

> “Example only: an excerpt must preserve meaning and context.”— Speaker or source type · retrieval method

Quote**≠**[Typography](/en/typography)

quote is sourced or deliberately highlighted original wording. [Typography](/en/typography) is the overall system of text hierarchy and style.

### When to use it

- A direct quote in an article

  Help people understand first, then take action.
- Authorized customer feedback
- A sourced principle excerpt

### When NOT to use it

- Every paragraph styled as a quote

  Help people understand first, then take action.
- Someone else’s view with no source or context
- A fictional person, publication, or review
