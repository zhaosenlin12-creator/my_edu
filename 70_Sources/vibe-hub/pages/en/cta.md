# CTA

You might say

Add a prominent spot on the page that makes people want to click 'Start free trial.'

**Tell visitors the next action you want them to take**·A call to action turns the page's message into a concrete next step, such as Start free, Book a demo, or Download. The wording should describe the result, and the action should appear after enough context to make the choice reasonable.
